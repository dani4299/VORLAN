Careers at DSRF Softech Studios

Join a small studio that builds software and AI automation for clients and for its own products. We value integrity, equality and diversity, and we look for people who care about craft.

Work that matters, with people who care

A small team means your work ships and your voice counts, on client projects and on our own product, VORLAN.

Meaningful problems

AI automation, custom systems and self-hosted software: work with a clear purpose and visible results.

Modern engineering
