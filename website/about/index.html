About DSRF Softech Studios

We make digital products with talent, tools, lots of passion and love.

Who we are

DSRF Softech Studios is a software development studio founded in 2025. We are a small team that designs and builds reliable, clean software and systems: websites and online stores, backend platforms, MVPs and AI automations.

What we want to hand over is more than code. It is an experience that is well designed and that you can trust to keep working.

What we work on

Our projects range from a single landing page to a full system with its own back end: AI automation, websites and e-commerce, MVPs, website performance and local-first software. The details are on the services page.

Our own products

We also build our own software. VORLAN is a self-hosted home server platform with storage, cameras, notes and a local AI assistant that work without the cloud, and it is where we test ideas we later bring to client work. Read the VORLAN case study.
